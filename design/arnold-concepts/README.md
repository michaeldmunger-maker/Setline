## Prompt briefs

### Who is Arnold?

Create a premium landscape UI and mascot exploration board for Setline. Use ink black #0B0E11, charcoal #14191F, lime #CCFF55 and restrained violet/blue. Present three distinct original friendly characters in minimal mobile welcome screens: a human gym buddy with dark curly hair, a lime anthropomorphic weight plate, and a charcoal athletic robot with an expressive lime face. Use a consistent tactile 3D visual language. No real-person likeness. Each welcome screen says “Hey, I’m Arnold.” and “Got any workouts to bring with you?” with “Bring my workouts” and “Start fresh” actions. Keep the screens spacious, approachable and free of dashboard clutter.

### A little at a time

Create a high-fidelity landscape board with three iPhone screens using the same dark/lime brand. Arnold is a friendly lime weight-plate mascot with expressive eyes, short athletic arms, black gloves, charcoal shorts and sneakers. Screen 1 introduces him, offering “Paste my notes”, “Upload a photo” and “I’m starting fresh”; no navigation or settings. Screen 2 uses a short conversation to turn “Weighted pull-ups / +10 lb / 12, 11, 10” into one editable draft; explain that missing dates can be saved as a routine. Screen 3 focuses on Today with a Chest & back workout, 6 exercises, a prominent Start workout action, a small optional food-tracking invitation, and only Today, Progress and Arnold navigation. Avoid charts, streaks, calendar grids, technical settings and dense forms. Title: “A little at a time.” Caption: “More tools appear when you need them.”

## Organization principle

First establish a workout the person can use. Keep import review to the details that need attention. After setup, emphasize the next workout. Offer food tracking and scheduling when requested, while keeping saved progress accessible. Additional features should not become compulsory setup work.
